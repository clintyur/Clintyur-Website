Crispy Carnitas Tacos

Slow-braised pork shoulder crisped in its own fat, served on warm corn tortillas with pickled onions and salsa verde.

🕐 Prep: 20m
🕐 Cook: 4h
👥 Serves: 6
👨‍🍳 Skill: Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 3 lbpork shoulder , cut in 3" chunks
  • 1 tbspkosher salt
  • 1 tspblack pepper
  • 1 tspcumin
  • 1 tspdried oregano
  • 4 clovesgarlic , smashed
  • 1orange , halved
  • 1 cuplard or neutral oil
  • 16corn tortillas , warmed
Per serving
520
kcal
38g
protein
28g
fat
32g
carbs

Method

  1. 1

    Season pork chunks all over with salt, pepper, cumin, and oregano. Let sit at room temperature for 30 minutes, or refrigerate uncovered overnight for best results.

  2. 2

    In a Dutch oven or heavy pot, combine pork, garlic, and lard over medium heat. Squeeze orange over pork, then drop in the spent halves.

  3. 3

    Braise uncovered on medium-low for 2–2.5 hours, turning occasionally, until pork is very tender and fat has rendered out.

  4. 4

    Increase heat to medium-high. Fry pork in its own fat, stirring occasionally, until golden and crispy on the outside — about 15–20 minutes.

  5. 5

    Transfer crispy pork to a cutting board. Shred roughly with two forks. Taste and adjust seasoning.

  6. 6

    Serve on warm corn tortillas with pickled red onions, salsa verde, fresh cilantro, and lime wedges.
